# Functional audit — October 8, 2026

Inspected the authenticated production Debts and Transactions screens and the current `codex/mobile-payments-budget` source. The fetched remote revision `48cc5fc` has the same source tree as the latest local deployed-work checkout `a134409`. Existing colors, fonts, icons, CSS, navigation and removed-screen visibility are preserved. No production financial records or authentication settings were changed.

## Verified issues and changes

- Editing a transaction/adjustment overwrote its displayed before/after balances with the correction's net application balances. The current amount could therefore appear next to an unchanged balance. New immutable application events describe each create/edit/delete/restore and every affected account. Prior versions remain retained. Legacy balance pairs are shown only for uncorrected entries whose captured difference agrees with the movement, labelled as balances captured when saved rather than historical lender balances. Backdating does not invent a date-ordered balance trail. Transactions exposes correction effects and prior versions; Debts activity also suppresses unsupported pairs.
- The payoff calculator's amount is the entire monthly pool, with minimums zeroed for allocation. Its label now says “Total monthly debt payment.” The explanation identifies included unarchived accounts, excluded separate house/car trackers, interest assumptions, no future purchases, no reserved lender minimums, and independence from Budget and past payments. Saved amounts are unchanged.
- Due dates are stored as complete calendar dates with date inputs, not recurring day-of-month fields. They remain unchanged and are labelled saved statement dates, without an overdue assertion or automatic rollover. Lender checks now save their effective date, including confirmations of unchanged balances. Household save status is explicitly distinguished from lender verification.
- Entry sign is the sole balance-direction control. Ordinary negatives default to payments. Optional refunds/credits reduce balances but are excluded from confirmed payment totals, Budget payment progress and payoff payment context. Editing a lender reconciliation does not automatically convert a decrease into a payment: that is an explicit reporting choice. Titles do not establish transaction classification. Restoring/correcting an entry is rejected if it would hide an overpayment behind zero-balance clamping.
- Progress uses a fixed account set when a saved check establishes one, retaining archived accounts and excluding later additions from that comparison. Missing original accounts prevent a repayment claim. Existing saved account baselines are retained; absent baselines can use an actual saved check. Missing starting dates or original membership are disclosed. Monthly check updates now preserve prior versions, and comparisons retain the earliest preserved account set. Charts do not join checks with different account sets as though they represent repayments.
- Recurring Budget copying already excludes explicit nonrecurring/one-time entries. Budget plans do not post lender movements. Linked loan payments already subtract estimated monthly interest and escrow before principal, reject duplicate regular monthly payments, and distinguish payoff quotes from principal. Added stale loan-payment form detection and disabled exposed debt/loan/Progress mutation controls for viewers.
- Household help now describes Google sign-in. Existing server-side role checks and atomic revision checks reject viewer writes and stale household revisions; existing serialized pending-save recovery retains conflicts rather than silently replacing remote data.

## Historical limits requiring a decision

The production “SFC Henderson - grocery” entry is stored as a +$66.96 reconciliation adjustment. Its title does not prove that the entire lender adjustment represents a purchase. Its balance impact and classification were preserved. A reporting-only purchase correction would require the household's confirmation; posting a second purchase would double-count the increase.

Old dates, inferred/imported baseline provenance, and lender balances cannot be verified from a household save. Prior corrections without application events cannot establish their full historical balance trail. Earlier monthly checks overwritten before this change cannot be recovered from absent records. No financial history was rewritten to fill these gaps.
